# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want imagen to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their experience level,
background, identity or ability. We pledge to act in ways that help build an open, friendly and
constructive community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent; ask clarifying questions before criticising.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and say what you'd do instead.
- Respect that people have different tools, setups and constraints.
- Credit others' ideas and effort.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in any form or medium
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory behaviour
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, commits and other contributions that don't follow
this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@tawanike](https://github.com/tawanike) through GitHub
(a private message or a [private security advisory](https://github.com/tawanike/imagen/security/advisories/new)
if you need confidentiality). Reports will be handled promptly and privately, and reporters will not be
retaliated against.
